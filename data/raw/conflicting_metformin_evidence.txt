Title: Conflicting Evidence Example: Metformin in Severe CKD

A small retrospective study reported that carefully monitored patients with severe chronic kidney disease who continued metformin did not experience increased adverse events.

However, this finding should be interpreted cautiously:
- sample size was small
- patients were treated in a specialized setting
- selection bias was likely
- the cohort may not represent the broader CKD population
- the finding conflicts with more conservative guidance for eGFR below 30

Validation implication:
If an AI-generated insight claims that metformin is broadly safe in all patients with eGFR below 30, the system should not mark that claim as fully validated. A more appropriate status may be weakly supported or rejected depending on the retrieved evidence and cohort context.

This document is intentionally included to test contradiction handling and weak evidence detection.
